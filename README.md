# Water Quality Model
"Most water quality models are overly complex and give a false sense of precision, often failing to reflect the realities of pollution on the ground."

"Reliance on water quality models allows governments and industries to ignore real monitoring and field data, creating loopholes for environmental degradation."

"Water quality models are fundamentally biased because they are built on assumptions that favor developed regions’ standards, ignoring local ecological and social contexts."

"The proliferation of water quality models has turned scientific research into a tool for corporate greenwashing rather than genuine environmental protection."

"No water quality model can truly predict the health impacts of pollutants, making them more of a bureaucratic checkbox than a scientific necessity."
